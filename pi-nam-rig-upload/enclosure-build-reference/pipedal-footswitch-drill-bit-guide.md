# Drill bit guide — footswitch enclosure

Work top to bottom (smallest to largest). Pilot every punch mark first.

| Step | Bit | Qty | Holes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pilot (all holes) | ⌀2-3mm | 32 | every punched mark, before its listed bit below |
| 1 | ⌀3mm | 4 | OLED mounting screws |
| 2 | ⌀3.5mm (or ⌀3mm) | 2 | USB coupler mounting screws |
| 3 | ⌀5mm | 2 | PRESET LED, SNAPSHOT LED |
| 4 | ⌀7mm (~7.1mm) | 1 | Preset-browse encoder |
| 5 | ⌀12mm (step drill #1, 4-32mm) | 10 | RESET, TUNER/MUTE, SW1-6, PRESET, SNAPSHOT |
| 6 | ⌀24mm (step drill #1, 4-32mm) | 1 | USB coupler bore |

Total round holes: 20. Plus 1× OLED rectangular cutout (~58×30mm, not a drill bit — pilot + rotary
tool + file to line).

Cutting oil on every hole, especially the 12mm/24mm steps. Deburr as you go.
